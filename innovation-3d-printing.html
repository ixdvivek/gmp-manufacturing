Home / Innovation / 3D Printing 3D Printing Precision, on-demand production that shortens the distance between an idea and a testable part.
We're building 3D printing capability in-house — precision, on-demand production that shortens the distance between an idea and a testable part.
What We're Building Precision, on-demand manufacturing
Traditional tooling can take weeks to produce a single prototype. 3D printing lets us go from design file to physical part in days — whether that's a dosage form concept, a device housing, or a custom fixture for one of our production lines. It's early-stage capability, built to sit alongside our existing manufacturing — not replace it — giving our formulation and engineering teams a faster way to test ideas before committing to full tooling.
Where It's Heading From prototyping to production support
Early applications span rapid prototyping, custom fixtures for our production lines, and exploratory work on printed dosage formats. As the capability matures, we expect it to support faster iteration on device components and short-run tooling — reducing lead times for partners working through development and pilot stages.
